Mechanical sounds, any keyboard

ThockBoard replaces whatever your keyboard sounds like with the sound you want — a deep thock, a bright clack, a vintage typewriter.

Pick a sound from the library and start typing. Everything is generated on the fly, so nothing is recorded and nothing leaves your machine.

Best with headphones. Press Ctrl+Alt+M to mute.
